# 📖 Glossary — deep-ocean terms used in this hub

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Abyssal plain** | Flat, sediment-covered seafloor at ~3,000–6,000 m; most of the ocean floor |
| **AMOC** | Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation: the "conveyor" moving warm water north and cold, dense water south at depth |
| **BBNJ / High Seas Treaty** | UN agreement on Biodiversity Beyond National Jurisdiction (in force 17 Jan 2026) |
| **Bioluminescence** | Light made by living things; the main light source below ~1,000 m |
| **CCZ** | Clarion-Clipperton Zone: Pacific abyssal plain rich in polymetallic nodules |
| **Chemosynthesis** | Making food from chemical energy (e.g. H₂S, CH₄) instead of sunlight |
| **Cold seep** | Seafloor site where methane and sulfide-rich fluids leak out slowly |
| **Dark oxygen** | Contested claim that nodule fields produce O₂ without light (Sweetman et al. 2024) |
| **DSHMRA** | US Deep Seabed Hard Mineral Resources Act (1980), basis for NOAA permits |
| **eDNA** | Environmental DNA: genetic traces in water used to detect species |
| **GEBCO** | General Bathymetric Chart of the Oceans; publishes the global seafloor grid |
| **Hadal zone** | Trenches deeper than ~6,000 m (to ~10,935 m) |
| **ISA** | International Seabed Authority, regulator of seabed minerals beyond national jurisdiction |
| **Marine snow** | Sinking organic particles that feed the deep sea |
| **Mesopelagic / twilight zone** | 200–1,000 m; dim light, huge daily vertical migrations |
| **Mining Code** | ISA's still-unfinished exploitation regulations |
| **Polymetallic nodule** | Potato-sized rock rich in Mn, Ni, Co, Cu; forms over millions of years |
| **ROV / AUV / HOV** | Remotely operated / autonomous / human-occupied vehicle |
| **Siboglinid** | Tubeworm family that relies on chemosynthetic symbiotic bacteria |
| **TMAO** | Trimethylamine N-oxide: osmolyte that stabilises proteins under pressure; limits fish to ~8,200–8,400 m |
| **Xenophyophore** | Giant single-celled organism common on abyssal plains |
