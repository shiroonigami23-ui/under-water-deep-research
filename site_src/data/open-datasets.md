---
title: Open Datasets and Portals
summary: Where to get real ocean data yourself, from bathymetry and floats to species records, video archives and protected-area maps.
level: Reference
---
| Dataset / portal | What it contains | Link |
|---|---|---|
| **GEBCO** grid | Global bathymetry, updated yearly (Seabed 2030) | <https://www.gebco.net> |
| **Argo** | ~4,000 profiling floats: temperature and salinity to 2,000 m (Deep Argo to 6,000 m) | <https://argo.ucsd.edu> |
| **OBIS** (Ocean Biodiversity Information System) | 100M+ species occurrence records | <https://obis.org> |
| **WoRMS** (World Register of Marine Species) | Authoritative list of marine species names | <https://www.marinespecies.org> |
| **FishBase** / **SeaLifeBase** | Biology of fishes and other marine life | <https://www.fishbase.se> |
| **NOAA Ocean Exploration** data and video | Okeanos Explorer dives, mapping data | <https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov> |
| **MBARI VARS** / **FathomNet** | Annotated deep-sea video and images for AI | <https://www.fathomnet.org> |
| **World Ocean Database** (NOAA NCEI) | Historical temperature, salinity and oxygen profiles | <https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/world-ocean-database> |
| **Copernicus Marine** | Ocean models and satellite products | <https://marine.copernicus.eu> |
| **NOAA Coral Reef Watch** | Satellite heat-stress and bleaching alerts | <https://coralreefwatch.noaa.gov> |
| **Protected Planet** (WDPA) | All protected areas, including MPAs | <https://www.protectedplanet.net> |
| **MPAtlas** | MPA protection levels | <https://mpatlas.org> |
| **Global Fishing Watch** | Fishing vessel activity from satellite tracking | <https://globalfishingwatch.org> |
| **ISA DeepData** | Environmental data from mining exploration contractors | <https://www.isa.org.jm/deepdata-database/> |
| **RAPID** array | AMOC transport at 26.5°N since 2004 | <https://rapid.ac.uk> |
| **Ocean Census NOVA** | New-species discovery records | <https://oceancensus.org> |

## Catalogs on this site

This encyclopedia's own species and seas data is open too: `site_src/data/species.json` and `site_src/data/seas.json` in the repository (MIT license). The [Species Atlas](/wiki/species/index.html) and [Seas & Oceans](/wiki/seas/index.html) are generated from them.
