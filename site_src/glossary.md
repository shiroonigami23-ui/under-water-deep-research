# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Abyssal plain** | Flat, sediment-covered seafloor at ~3,000–6,000 m; covers most of the ocean floor |
| **Abyssopelagic zone** | Open water from 4,000 to 6,000 m |
| **AMOC** | Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation: the "conveyor" carrying warm water north and cold, dense water south at depth |
| **Anoxic** | Without oxygen |
| **Aragonite saturation (Ω)** | Measure of how easily shell-building animals can form calcium carbonate; below 1, shells dissolve |
| **AUV / ROV / HOV** | Autonomous / remotely operated / human-occupied vehicle |
| **Bathypelagic zone** | Midnight zone, 1,000–4,000 m |
| **BBNJ / High Seas Treaty** | UN agreement on Biodiversity Beyond National Jurisdiction (in force 17 Jan 2026) |
| **Benthic** | Living on or in the seafloor |
| **Bioluminescence** | Light made by living things; the main light source below ~1,000 m |
| **Black smoker** | Hydrothermal vent emitting 350–400 °C fluid darkened by metal sulfides |
| **Brine pool** | Lake of extremely salty, dense water on the seafloor |
| **CCZ** | Clarion-Clipperton Zone: Pacific abyssal plain rich in polymetallic nodules |
| **Chemosynthesis** | Making food from chemical energy (H₂S, CH₄, H₂) instead of sunlight |
| **Cold seep** | Seafloor site where methane- and sulfide-rich fluids leak out slowly |
| **Counter-illumination** | Glowing belly light that hides an animal's silhouette from below |
| **Dark oxygen** | Contested claim that nodule fields produce O₂ without light |
| **Diel vertical migration** | Daily migration of animals up at night and down by day; the largest migration on Earth |
| **eDNA** | Environmental DNA: genetic traces in water used to detect species |
| **EEZ** | Exclusive Economic Zone, up to 200 nautical miles from the coast |
| **Endemic** | Found nowhere else |
| **Epipelagic zone** | Sunlit zone, 0–200 m |
| **GEBCO** | General Bathymetric Chart of the Oceans; publishes the global seafloor grid |
| **Hadal zone** | Trenches deeper than ~6,000 m (to ~10,935 m) |
| **Hydrothermal vent** | Seafloor hot spring at volcanic spreading centres |
| **ISA** | International Seabed Authority, regulator of seabed minerals beyond national jurisdiction |
| **Marine snow** | Sinking organic particles that feed the deep sea |
| **Mesopelagic / twilight zone** | 200–1,000 m; dim light, huge daily vertical migrations |
| **Mining Code** | The ISA's still-unfinished exploitation regulations |
| **MPA** | Marine protected area |
| **OHC** | Ocean heat content, the total heat stored in the ocean (measured in zettajoules) |
| **OMZ** | Oxygen minimum zone, a natural mid-water layer of very low oxygen |
| **Pelagic** | Living in open water, away from the bottom and shore |
| **Photophore** | Light-producing organ |
| **Piezolyte** | Molecule (such as TMAO) that protects proteins from pressure |
| **Polymetallic nodule** | Potato-sized lump rich in Mn, Ni, Co, Cu; grows over millions of years |
| **Seamount** | Underwater mountain rising at least 1,000 m above the seafloor |
| **Siboglinid** | Tubeworm family that relies on chemosynthetic symbiotic bacteria |
| **Subduction** | One tectonic plate diving beneath another; it forms trenches |
| **Thermocline** | Layer where temperature drops rapidly with depth |
| **TMAO** | Trimethylamine N-oxide: osmolyte that stabilises proteins under pressure; limits fish to ~8,200–8,400 m |
| **Turbidity current** | Underwater avalanche of sediment-laden water |
| **Upwelling** | Rise of cold, nutrient-rich deep water to the surface |
| **Whale fall** | Whale carcass on the seafloor that supports a decades-long ecosystem |
| **Xenophyophore** | Giant single-celled organism common on abyssal plains |
| **Zettajoule (ZJ)** | 10²¹ joules |
