| Platform | What it is | Strengths | Limits | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **HOV** (human-occupied vehicle) | Crewed submersible with a pressure sphere | Human observation, dexterous sampling, public impact | Expensive, short bottom times, risk | Alvin (to 6,500 m), Fendouzhe and Limiting Factor (full depth), Shinkai 6500, Matsya 6000 (India, in development) |
| **ROV** (remotely operated vehicle) | Tethered robot piloted from a ship | Long dives, high-power tools, live HD video, telepresence | Needs a large ship and tether | SuBastian, Isis, Okeanos Explorer's Deep Discoverer, MBARI's Doc Ricketts |
| **AUV** (autonomous underwater vehicle) | Untethered pre-programmed robot | High-resolution mapping close to the seafloor; wide coverage | Limited real-time control | Sentry, Autosub, Orpheus |
| **Lander** | Instrument frame dropped to the seafloor | Long-duration measurements, baited cameras, chemistry chambers | Fixed position | Hadal landers (deepest fish records), dark-oxygen chambers |
| **Floats and gliders** | Profiling floats and buoyancy-driven gliders | Global ocean monitoring for years | Mostly upper 2,000 m (Deep Argo to 6,000 m) | Argo network (~4,000 floats) |

## Engineering for pressure

- **Pressure hulls:** titanium spheres, often 7–9 cm thick for full ocean depth. Composite hulls have failed catastrophically (the Titan implosion in 2023).
- **Buoyancy:** syntactic foam made of glass microballoons in epoxy.
- **Oil-compensated systems** let motors and electronics sit in oil at ambient pressure instead of in heavy housings.
- **Communication:** acoustic modems underwater (low bandwidth), and fibre-optic tethers for ROVs.

## India's deep-ocean programme

India's **Deep Ocean Mission** includes **Samudrayaan**, which plans to carry three people to 6,000 m in the **Matsya 6000** submersible being developed by the National Institute of Ocean Technology. India also holds an ISA nodule exploration contract in the Central Indian Ocean Basin.

## Trends

- Telepresence: expeditions streamed live with remote scientists ashore.
- Low-cost cameras and landers to widen who explores.
- AI for automated species detection in video.
