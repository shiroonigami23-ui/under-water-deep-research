🐍 Pelican eel (gulper eel)

Eurypharynx pelecanoides

An eel that is mostly mouth: a loose pouch-like jaw much larger than its body.

Scientific nameEurypharynx pelecanoides
GroupDeep-sea fish
Min depth (m)500
Max depth (m)3000
WhereAtlantic, Pacific, Indian
SizeUp to ~1 m
LifespanUnknown
DietSmall crustaceans, fish
IUCN statusLeast Concern

Overview#

The pelican eel has an enormous, loosely hinged mouth, a whip-like tail tipped with a light organ, and tiny eyes.

Habitat and type of water#

Tropical and temperate oceans worldwide, bathypelagic.

Feeding and hunting#

Despite its huge mouth it eats mostly small crustaceans, scooping water and prey together and then expelling the water through its gills.

Mating and reproduction#

Males develop large olfactory organs at maturity, and adults are thought to die after spawning (semelparity), as in other eels.

Movement and migration#

Larvae (leptocephali) live in upper waters.

Behaviour and social life#

Can inflate its mouth like a balloon, possibly to deter predators or to engulf prey swarms.

Adaptations#

  • Massive gape
  • Bioluminescent tail tip possibly used as a lure

Conservation#

Not fished.
