Hadal life & chemosynthesis

Discovery of the deepest and most extensive chemosynthesis-based communities on Earth

Fendouzhe dives found tubeworm and bivalve communities fed by methane seeps across ~2,500 km of the Kuril–Kamchatka and Aleutian trenches, down to 9,533 m.

Deep-sea mining

Long-term impact and biological recovery in a deep-sea mining track

A 1979 nodule-collection test track still shows reduced animal life after 44 years, though some mobile fauna have returned.

Polymetallic nodules

Evidence of dark oxygen production at the abyssal seafloor

Benthic chambers on nodule fields recorded oxygen increasing in the dark; the authors propose seawater electrolysis on nodule surfaces. Contested.

Exploration

How little we've seen: a visual coverage estimate of the deep seafloor

Only ~0.001% of the deep seafloor has ever been imaged, mostly near the USA, Japan and New Zealand.

Climate & circulation

Observationally constrained projections of AMOC weakening

Projects a 43–59% AMOC weakening by 2100 under current trajectories, ~20% with strong emission cuts.

Ocean heat

Ocean heat content sets another record in 2025

Upper-2,000 m ocean heat rose by ~23 ZJ in 2025, the ninth straight record.

Acidification

Ocean acidification: another planetary boundary crossed

The global ocean entered the acidification boundary's uncertainty range by 2020; up to 60% of the upper 200 m has crossed it.

Deoxygenation

Decline in global oceanic oxygen content during the past five decades

Global ocean oxygen fell by more than 2% between 1960 and 2010.

Deoxygenation

Declining oxygen in the global ocean and coastal waters

Open-ocean oxygen-free water has quadrupled and coastal low-oxygen sites have increased more than tenfold since 1950.

Plastics & pollution

The deep sea is a major sink for microplastic debris

Microplastic fibres were up to four times more abundant in deep-sea sediments than in polluted surface waters.

Plastics & pollution

Deep-sea microplastic hotspots driven by turbidity currents

Direct observation of turbidity currents carrying microplastics down submarine canyons.

Protection & trawling

Protecting the global ocean for biodiversity, food and climate

Strategic protection of ~30% of the ocean could deliver large benefits; estimated trawling disturbs sediment carbon equivalent to ~1 Gt CO₂ a year.

Hadal life

Marine fish may be biochemically constrained from inhabiting the deepest ocean depths

TMAO rises linearly with depth and would make fish hyperosmotic near ~8,200 m, explaining the fish depth limit.

Coral bleaching

Global warming and recurrent mass bleaching of corals

Analysed the 2016 Great Barrier Reef bleaching and showed local protection did not prevent it.

Coral bleaching

Global warming transforms coral reef assemblages

The 2016 heatwave caused a catastrophic die-off and changed reef communities.

Species discovery

How many species are there on Earth and in the ocean?

Estimated ~2.2 million marine species, with ~91% still undescribed.

Hydrothermal vents

Deep-sea ecosystems: the hydrothermal vents of the Galápagos Rift

The first scientific description of hydrothermal vent communities discovered by Alvin in 1977.

Hydrothermal vents

An off-axis hydrothermal vent field near the Mid-Atlantic Ridge at 30° N (Lost City)

Discovery of the Lost City field of carbonate towers powered by serpentinisation.

Marine heatwaves

Global distribution of marine heatwaves / longer and more frequent marine heatwaves

Marine heatwave days increased by ~54% from 1925 to 2016.

Seafloor mapping

The GEBCO_2024 grid and Seabed 2030 progress

The global bathymetric grid used to track how much of the seafloor is mapped.

Deep circulation

Rapid deep-ocean warming and Antarctic Bottom Water changes

Showed the abyssal ocean is warming, contributing to sea-level rise.

Deep-sea mining

Abundance and diversity of deep-sea fauna in the Clarion-Clipperton Zone

Checklist of ~5,500 species recorded in the CCZ, ~90% undescribed.
