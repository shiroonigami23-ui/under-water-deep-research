🌊 Gulf of Aden

Arabian Sea

A young rifting gulf linking the Red Sea to the Indian Ocean.

TypeGulf
Part ofArabian Sea
Area (km²)410,000
Average depth (m)1800
Max depth (m)5390
Deepest pointCentral rift

🧭 At a glance

Gulf of Aden covers about 410,000 km², averages 1,800 m deep and reaches 5,390 m at Central rift. This encyclopedia links 20 species to it, including Emperor angelfish, Greater blue-ringed octopus, Humphead wrasse, Threadfin butterflyfish, Arabian Sea humpback whale, Arabian angelfish, Beaked sea snake, Bombay duck.

Overview#

A deep gulf between Yemen and Somalia linking the Red Sea to the Arabian Sea through the Bab-el-Mandeb strait.

Geography#

Bounded by Yemen, Djibouti and Somalia.

Seafloor and geology#

A young spreading ridge reaching about 5,390 m.

Water and currents#

Monsoon upwelling.

Climate, weather and cyclones#

Desert climate with monsoon winds.

Flora and fauna#

Sharks, dolphins and reefs.

Ecosystems#

Coral reefs off Socotra.

History and exploration#

Ancient incense and spice route; modern piracy hotspot.

People and economy#

A shipping lane carrying Suez traffic.

Threats and protection#

Piracy (peaked 2008–2011) and attacks on shipping since 2023.
