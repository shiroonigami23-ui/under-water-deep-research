🌊 Seas & Oceans

All five oceans and the world's major seas: size, depth, deepest point, salinity, currents, life, people and threats.

26 entries in 5 groups.

Ocean

Pacific Ocean

The largest and deepest ocean, covering about a third of Earth's surface.

Ocean

Atlantic Ocean

The second-largest ocean, split down the middle by the Mid-Atlantic Ridge and home to the AMOC.

Ocean

Indian Ocean

The warmest ocean, ruled by monsoon reversals of winds and currents.

Ocean

Southern Ocean

The ring of ocean around Antarctica, driven by the strongest current on Earth.

Ocean

Arctic Ocean

The smallest, shallowest ocean, losing its summer sea ice fast.

Sea

Mediterranean Sea

A nearly enclosed sea with unusually warm, salty deep water and a long human history.

Sea

Caribbean Sea

A tropical sea of coral reefs and the deepest point of the Caribbean, the Cayman Trough.

Gulf

Gulf of Mexico

A basin of natural oil seeps, brine pools and a large seasonal 'dead zone'.

Sea

Sargasso Sea

The only sea without coastlines, defined by currents and floating Sargassum seaweed.

Sea

North Sea

A shallow, heavily used shelf sea between Britain and mainland Europe.

Sea

Baltic Sea

A young brackish sea with some of the largest dead zones on Earth.

Sea

Black Sea

The largest anoxic body of water on Earth: below ~150–200 m there is no oxygen.

Sea

Red Sea

A young ocean in the making, with heat-tolerant corals and hot brine pools.

Sea

Arabian Sea

Home to one of the world's thickest oxygen minimum zones, driven by monsoon upwelling.

Bay

Bay of Bengal

The world's largest bay, freshened by the Ganges–Brahmaputra and fuelling strong cyclones.

Sea

Coral Sea

Home to the Great Barrier Reef, the largest coral reef system on Earth.

Sea

South China Sea

A biodiverse and contested sea crossed by a large share of global shipping.

Sea

Sea of Japan (East Sea)

A semi-enclosed sea that forms its own deep water, like a mini-ocean.

Sea

Bering Sea

One of the most productive seas on Earth, supporting huge pollock and crab fisheries.

Sea

Weddell Sea

A major source of Antarctic Bottom Water and the resting place of Shackleton's Endurance.

Sea

Ross Sea

Often called the most pristine marine ecosystem left, and protected by a large MPA since 2017.

Gulf

Persian Gulf

A hot, hyper-saline shallow sea whose corals survive extremes that kill corals elsewhere.

Sea

Tasman Sea

Between Australia and New Zealand, a marine-heatwave hotspot.

Bay

Hudson Bay

A huge inland sea where polar bears depend on seasonal ice.

Sea

Barents Sea

The Arctic's 'hot spot' of warming, where Atlantic water is pushing north.

Lake-sea (endorheic)

Caspian Sea

The largest enclosed inland body of water, salty but not connected to the ocean.
