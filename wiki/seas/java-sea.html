🌊 Java Sea

Pacific / Indian

A shallow equatorial sea in the heart of Indonesia.

TypeSea
Part ofPacific / Indian
Area (km²)320,000
Average depth (m)46
Max depth (m)203
Deepest pointEast of Bawean
Salinity~32–33 PSU

🧭 At a glance

Java Sea covers about 320,000 km², averages 46 m deep and reaches 203 m at East of Bawean. This encyclopedia links 211 species to it, including Banded pipefish, Blue tang, Coconut crab, Emperor angelfish, Flamboyant cuttlefish, Giant frogfish, Humphead wrasse, Indo-Pacific sailfish. This encyclopedia links 1 rivers to it, including Irrawaddy. Wrecks and discoveries here: Belitung shipwreck.

Overview#

Part of the Sunda Shelf, dry land in the ice age.

Geography#

Between Java, Borneo and Sumatra.

Seafloor and geology#

Very shallow shelf.

Water and currents#

Monsoon-reversing currents.

Climate, weather and cyclones#

Equatorial; heavy rainfall.

Flora and fauna#

Coral reefs (Karimunjawa), mangroves.

Ecosystems#

Shelf ecosystems.

History and exploration#

Battle of the Java Sea (1942); many WWII wrecks, some illegally salvaged.

People and economy#

Fisheries, shipping.

Threats and protection#

Pollution, overfishing.
