🌊 Beaufort Sea

Arctic Ocean

An Arctic sea north of Alaska and Canada, home to the Beaufort Gyre.

TypeSea

🧭 At a glance

Beaufort Sea covers about 476,000 km², averages 1,004 m deep and reaches 4,683 m at Canada Basin margin. This encyclopedia links 37 species to it, including Atlantic cod, Greenland shark, Narwhal, Adélie penguin, Antarctic brittle star, Antarctic sea star, Arctic char, Bar-tailed godwit.

Overview#

An Arctic sea north of Alaska and Canada.

Geography#

Bounded by Alaska’s North Slope and the Canadian Arctic Archipelago.

Seafloor and geology#

Shelf and the deep Canada Basin.

Water and currents#

The Beaufort Gyre stores Arctic freshwater.

Climate, weather and cyclones#

Sea ice most of the year, now melting further each summer.

Flora and fauna#

Bowhead whales, belugas, polar bears and ringed seals.

Ecosystems#

Sea-ice and coastal lagoons.

History and exploration#

Search for the Northwest Passage.

People and economy#

Iñupiat and Inuvialuit communities; oil fields at Prudhoe Bay.

Threats and protection#

Sea-ice loss and oil development.
