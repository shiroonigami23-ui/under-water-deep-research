USS Indianapolis

Yamato

Cargo ship

A famous dive site filled with WWII motorbikes, trucks and trains.

Truk Lagoon fleet

Over 50 ships sunk in Operation Hailstone, now a reef-covered underwater museum.

Batavia

Wrecked off Australia, followed by a notorious mutiny and massacre.

The largest ship lost on the Great Lakes, in a November storm.
