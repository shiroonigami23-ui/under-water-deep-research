🏞 Rivers & Estuaries

The great rivers that feed the ocean: their course, seasonal flood cycles, flora and fauna, people and threats, linked to the seas they drain into and the species that live there.

Amazon

The largest river by volume, carrying about a fifth of all river water that reaches the ocean.

Nile

Often cited as the longest river, the lifeline of Egyptian civilisation.

Asia

Yangtze

Asia's longest river and home to the Three Gorges Dam.

Mississippi–Missouri

North America's largest river system, draining ~40% of the contiguous USA.

Yenisei

The largest river flowing into the Arctic Ocean.

Lena

Flows through permafrost to a vast Arctic delta.

Congo

The deepest river in the world (over 220 m) and second by discharge.

Asia

Mekong

The world's largest inland fishery, flowing through six countries.

Asia

Ganges (Ganga)

India's sacred river, forming with the Brahmaputra the world's largest delta.

Brahmaputra

A braided giant flowing from Tibet through Assam and Bangladesh.

Asia

Indus

Cradle of the Harappan civilisation.

Asia

Godavari

India's second-longest river, the 'Dakshin Ganga'.

Mahanadi

Odisha's great river, feeding Chilika Lake area and the Bhitarkanika mangroves.

Asia

Krishna

A major Deccan river with mangroves at its mouth.

Asia

Narmada

A west-flowing rift-valley river sacred in central India.

Asia

Irrawaddy

Myanmar's lifeline, home of the Irrawaddy dolphin.

Africa

Zambezi

Africa's fourth-longest river, plunging over Victoria Falls.

Africa

Niger

Flows in a boomerang through the Sahel to a vast oil-rich delta.

Europe

Danube

Europe's second-longest river, flowing through 10 countries.

Europe

Volga

Europe's longest river.

Europe

Rhine

Western Europe's busiest waterway.

Europe

Thames

London's river, once declared biologically dead (1957), now home to seals.

North America

St. Lawrence

Outlet of the Great Lakes, with one of the world's largest estuaries.

North America

Columbia

Historically the greatest salmon river on Earth.

North America

Yukon

Home to one of the longest salmon migrations.

South America

Orinoco

Venezuela's great river with a vast delta.

South America

Paraná

South America's second-largest river.

Oceania

Murray–Darling

Australia's largest river system.

Fly

Asia

Amur

The twin rivers of Mesopotamia.
